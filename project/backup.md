The product is easy to understand from the campaign page: it is a seven-pound, skin-on-frame kayak with a carbon-fiber frame that packs down to approximately the size of a small tent and can be assembled in about five to seven minutes. The campaign provides a clear price, with the standard Packable Kayak reward offered for $625. The campaign is still in the funding stage, so the product has not yet been delivered to Kickstarter backers.
