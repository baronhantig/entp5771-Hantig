# Customer Channels
## Channel 1: My Fraternity
My first channel will be my fraternity at the University of Utah. I have direct access to approximately 120 members, primarily college-aged men who could be potential customers for a slim, compact everyday wallet. I plan to approach members in person at our fraternity chapter meeting, which meets every Monday at 6:00 p.m. I will ask members face-to-face if they are willing to participate in my class research and then text the survey link to those who agree. Because I am a member of the fraternity and regularly attend these meetings, I already have direct access to this group.
## Channel 2: Reddit — r/SampleSize
My second channel will be r/SampleSize, a Reddit community created for posting surveys and recruiting research participants. The community has approximately 247,000 members, although I will specifically target adults ages 18–25 who could realistically consider purchasing an everyday wallet. I will post my survey at approximately 7:00 p.m. Mountain Time on the first weekday of my assigned data-collection period and leave it available throughout the collection period. I will aim to receive at least 25 qualified responses from this channel. I can access these respondents by following the community’s posting requirements, labeling the survey as academic research, and clearly identifying the required age group. This channel will help me reach potential customers outside my fraternity and the University of Utah.
