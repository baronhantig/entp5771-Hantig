The largest drop occurs before respondents begin the survey: only 11 of
124 fraternity recipients and 31 of 270 Reddit viewers started, while
most people who started passed the screener and finished.

This suggests that access and motivating people to begin the survey are
bigger problems than the screener or survey length, and Reddit has been
substantially more productive than the fraternity channel.

Over the next ten days, I will add direct, face-to-face recruiting to
increase participation outside Reddit while continuing to collect
responses through both existing channels.
