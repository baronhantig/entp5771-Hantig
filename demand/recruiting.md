The fraternity gives me direct, in-person access to likely wallet users. Reddit will provide a separate group outside my fraternity and university. I will record the recruiting channel for each response so I can compare the two groups instead of treating them as one identical market.

If the Reddit post is removed or receives little activity, I will revise it to meet the community’s requirements and repost it during another weekday evening. If it remains unavailable, I will replace it with direct outreach through another University of Utah student group after receiving permission from its leader.

## **2\. Exact Ask**

### **In-Person Ask**

> Hi, I’m a University of Utah student conducting a short, anonymous survey for a class project about wallets and cardholders. I’m looking for adults who choose the wallet or cardholder they use. If you’re 18 or older, would you be willing to take about three minutes to complete it on your phone? I can text you the link now and share a brief summary of the findings after the survey closes.

### **Reddit Post**

**Title:** Academic survey about wallets and cardholders — adults 18 and older

> I am a University of Utah student trying to learn how adults value a modular card wallet and whether it is worth building. If you are 18 or older and choose the wallet or cardholder you use, I would appreciate your response to this anonymous survey. It should take about three minutes. I will add a brief summary of the findings to this post after the survey closes.

## **3\. What Respondents Receive**

Respondents will be offered a brief summary of the findings after the survey closes. I will share the summary in my fraternity’s group communication channel and add a results link to the original Reddit post. The demand survey will not collect names, email addresses, or other identifying information.

Respondents will not receive cash, a wallet, a discount, or another product-based incentive.
