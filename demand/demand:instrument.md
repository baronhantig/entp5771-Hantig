## **Section 3 \- Describe the Offering**

### **Image**

**Insert the single neutral product-only image specified in the form setup. Do not include any visible price, discount, backer total, slogan, or reward-tier language.**

### **Respondent-facing product description**

> The product being evaluated is one Bare Bones Ohsnap Wallet 2\. It is a modular card wallet made from two aluminum covers and one interchangeable card core. The buyer can select a core intended to hold 3, 5, or 7 cards and can choose the wallet color. Pulling a lever causes the cards to fan out for access.

> This Bare Bones configuration is designed to carry cards. It does not include the MagSafe phone-mounting feature, additional cash or storage components, or premium cover materials offered with other configurations. The item being evaluated is one Bare Bones wallet in the buyer's selected card capacity and color.
