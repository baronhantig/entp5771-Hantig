# **Modular Card Wallet Demand Instrument**

## **Form setup (not shown to respondents)**

* **Form tool:** Google Forms  
* **Status:** Build and preview only. Do not publish or field until approved.  
* **Response settings:** Do not record names or email addresses. Do not require sign-in if that would identify respondents.  
* **Question requirements:** All questions are required unless marked optional.  
* **Branching:** Any disqualifying answer in Section 1 routes directly to the ineligible end message.  
* **Currency:** All monetary responses are in U.S. dollars.  
* **Image:** In Section 3, insert one neutral product-only image of the modular card wallet. Crop out or remove any visible brand name or logo, Kickstarter price, MSRP, discount, backer count, slogans, and reward text. The image should show the wallet itself and its aluminum-cover/card-core construction without showing accessories from other configurations.

* No

* Yes

## **Section 3 \- Describe the Offering**

### **Image**

**Insert one neutral product-only image as specified in the form setup. Do not include any visible brand name or logo, price, discount, backer total, slogan, or reward-tier language.**

### **Respondent-facing product description**

> The product being evaluated is one bare-bones modular card wallet. It is made from two aluminum covers and one interchangeable card core. The buyer can select a core intended to hold 3, 5, or 7 cards and can choose the wallet color. Pulling a lever causes the cards to fan out for access.

> This bare-bones configuration is designed to carry cards. It does not include phone-mounting features, additional cash or storage components, or premium cover materials offered with other configurations. The item being evaluated is one bare-bones modular card wallet in the buyer's selected card capacity and color.
