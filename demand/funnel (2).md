# Milestone I: Demand — Recruiting Funnel

## Recruiting channels and measurement

I recruited respondents through two channels: my University of Utah fraternity and a post in Reddit's r/SampleSize. Both channels used the same Google Form link, and the form did not separately record the recruitment source.

For the fraternity, I counted 124 people approached and identified 9 completed responses from members I knew had taken the survey. For Reddit, the updated Post Insights showed 311 post views, up from 270 at the previous checkpoint. A view is exposure to the post, not proof that a unique person opened the survey. I used no other recruitment channels. I therefore attribute the remaining 30 usable responses to Reddit by elimination, not through individual source tracking.

## Funnel counts

| Stage | Fraternity | Reddit | Combined |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Approached / exposed | 124 | 311 | 435 |
| Started | Not measurable separately | Not measurable separately | 42 submitted form responses* |
| Passed screener | Not measurable separately | Not measurable separately | 39 |
| Finished with usable willingness-to-pay answer | 9 | 30 (inferred) | 39 |

*The 42 figure counts submitted Google Form response rows, not every person who opened or began filling out the form. Because Google Forms did not track incomplete, unsubmitted attempts, actual starts may have been higher.

The original export contains 42 submitted rows. Three failed or did not complete the screening requirements and had no willingness-to-pay answer. The remaining 39 passed the three screeners and provided numeric willingness-to-pay answers, including eight genuine $0 answers. One eligible respondent left the product-familiarity question blank, so “finished” here means a complete usable response for the demand analysis, not necessarily every optional field answered. See `cleaning.md`.

## Conversion measures and limitations

| Measure | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Fraternity usable finishes per approached | 9 / 124 | 7.3% |
| Reddit inferred usable finishes per post view | 30 / 311 | 9.6% |
| Combined usable finishes per recorded exposure | 39 / 435 | 9.0% |
| Eligible usable responses per submitted form | 39 / 42 | 92.9% |

These ratios are descriptive, not independently verified funnel-stage conversion rates. In particular, Reddit post views may include repeat views, and the 124 fraternity approaches and 311 Reddit views are not necessarily comparable measures of unique people. Because both channels used the same link without a source question, I cannot report channel-specific Starts or Passed Screener without inventing numbers.

## Fraternity — three-sentence checkpoint

Of 124 fraternity members approached, 9 provided usable completed surveys, but I cannot see how many started the form or were screened out. The 7.3% usable-response-to-approach ratio suggests that getting members to take the survey was a larger observable obstacle than obtaining usable answers from those who submitted it, although the missing stages prevent a precise diagnosis. In the next ten days, I would recruit members face-to-face and record how many actually open the form, pass the screeners, and finish.

## Reddit — three-sentence checkpoint

The Reddit post received 311 views, while 30 usable responses are attributed to Reddit because they were not among the 9 known fraternity responses, but I cannot observe Reddit-specific starts or screener passes. The 9.6% inferred usable-response-to-view ratio suggests the post generated some participation, although views are not unique people and I cannot locate the exact point of drop-off. In the next ten days, I would track new post insights and use a separate channel-specific link or a recruitment-source question to measure the Reddit funnel more accurately.

## Interpretation

The updated funnel records 435 approaches or post views across the two channels and 39 eligible, usable survey responses. It is not possible to reconstruct every stage by channel from a shared Google Form link, and the 42 submitted forms should not be misrepresented as all people who started the survey. These limitations matter when interpreting the reachable population and comparing the stated willingness to pay with the Ohsnap campaign's actual backers.
