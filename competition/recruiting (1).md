# Competition Survey — Recruiting Plan

## Goal and eligibility

My goal is **30 completed, eligible responses** to the competition survey by November 22, 2026. This is a planning target, not a result or a claim about the number of responses already collected. Respondents must be at least 18, personally choose their wallet or card holder, and carry physical cards at least occasionally. **Nobody who completed my earlier wallet survey should take this new survey.**

## Channels and numerical plan

I plan to recruit through two channels: (1) new people reached through my University of Utah fraternity network and (2) Reddit's r/SampleSize. I will not recontact or invite known participants from the first survey. I will make the no-repeat requirement explicit in every invitation.

In my previous survey, the fraternity channel produced 9 usable finishes from 124 approaches, or **9/124 = 7.3%**. Reddit produced approximately 30 usable finishes from 311 post views, or **30/311 = 9.6%**. The Reddit finish count was inferred from the overall total rather than tracked individually, and views are not necessarily unique people; these are rough planning rates, not measured conversion rates for this new survey.

A provisional split is **10 eligible finishes from the fraternity channel and 20 from Reddit**. At the prior rates, this implies approximately **10 ÷ (9/124) = 138 fraternity approaches** and **20 ÷ (30/311) = 208 Reddit post views**, or about **346 total channel exposures**. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed outcomes. Because this survey is longer and excludes prior respondents, I may need substantially more outreach and will reassess after the first few days.

## Tracking and attribution

To avoid the attribution problem in my first survey, I will use a channel-identification method before launch: either distinct prefilled links with a required source field or a required question asking where respondents found the survey. I will test that the source information is saved correctly and cannot easily be skipped. If I cannot distinguish channels reliably, I will report the limitation instead of assigning finishes by subtraction.

For **each channel**, I will record the date and the actual number **approached**, **started**, **passed screener**, and **finished** in `competition/funnel.md`. For direct outreach, approached means people actually invited or demonstrably exposed to the request, not everyone in a fraternity. For Reddit, I will report post views/impressions if available and label them as views rather than unique people. Standard Google Forms does not directly report how many people started but abandoned a form; unless I have another defensible measure, I will mark **Started: not measurable** rather than inventing a number.

I will begin tracking on the day I launch, update the funnel during recruitment, and close the survey on November 22, 2026. The actual launch date, link(s), outreach counts, and responses will be added after they occur.
