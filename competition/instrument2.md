## 2. Context

**Form description:** Academic research survey comparing two card wallets. Participation is voluntary. The products are examples for a class project; your answers do not determine whether either product will be produced. Do not enter identifying information. Please answer for one 12-month period.

**About this survey:** This class survey asks about two types of card wallets, their appeal, and how many you might choose at different prices. Neither product is being offered for sale here. There are no right answers; zero is a useful answer. Think about your own needs, not what you think the researcher wants.

## 3. The two wallets

**Wallet A**

A compact card wallet designed to carry a small set of physical cards. It uses a built-in mechanism to make cards accessible and is intended for everyday pocket carry. The version being compared is the basic card-wallet version, without optional accessories.

**Wallet B**

A compact aluminum card wallet designed to carry up to six physical cards. It uses a built-in mechanism that slides cards outward for access and is intended for everyday pocket carry. The version being compared is the basic card-wallet version, without optional accessories.

**Implementation note (not shown):** Wallet A = Bare Bones Ohsnap Wallet 2; Wallet B = Secrid Cardprotector. Product claims need verification. No images should be shown unless both products receive comparable neutral images. Delete the internal image reminder before launch.

## 5. Prices and purchase choices

**Your maximum prices**

Enter the maximum amount you would be willing to pay for each wallet in whole dollars. Enter 0 if you would not pay anything.

Remember both amounts you enter. In the next questions, we will refer to these as your "Wallet A maximum price" and "Wallet B maximum price."

**Q8. What is the most you would pay for Wallet A per 12 months? (USD)** (Required; short answer; numeric validation 0–1000)

**Q9. What is the most you would pay for Wallet B per 12 months? (USD)** (Required; short answer; numeric validation 0–1000)

**Quantity scenarios**

For each scenario, imagine the two prices shown apply at the same time. “Your Wallet A maximum” and “your Wallet B maximum” mean the two numbers you JUST entered. Answer how many of each wallet you would buy over 12 months. Enter 0 if you would buy none. The scenarios are hypothetical and may not reflect real-world pricing.

**How many Wallet A would you buy?** (Heading only)

**Q10. Wallet A — Condition 1:** Wallet A is permanently free; Wallet B is permanently free. How many Wallet A would you buy per 12 months? (Required; nonnegative whole number)

**Q11. Wallet A — Condition 2:** Wallet A costs YOUR maximum price for A; Wallet B is free. How many Wallet A would you buy per 12 months? (Required; nonnegative whole number)

**Q12. Wallet A — Condition 3:** Wallet A is free; Wallet B costs YOUR maximum price for B. How many Wallet A would you buy per 12 months? (Required; nonnegative whole number)

**Q13. Wallet A — Condition 4:** Wallet A costs YOUR maximum price for A; Wallet B costs YOUR maximum price for B. How many Wallet A would you buy per 12 months? (Required; nonnegative whole number)

**How many Wallet B would you buy?** (Heading only)
