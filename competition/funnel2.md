## Definitions and evidence

- **Approached — fraternity:** Number of people actually contacted or reliably exposed to the new invitation; do not substitute the fraternity's total membership unless the entire membership was demonstrably reached.
- **Approached — Reddit:** Actual views/impressions of the new survey post, if available. Label these as views rather than unique individuals. If unavailable, mark as not measurable.
- **Started:** People who opened and began the survey. Google Forms does not ordinarily report starts before submission, so mark this as **not measurable** unless Baron has independent reliable evidence.
- **Passed screener:** Respondents who submitted the new form and answered Yes to all three eligibility questions (18+, chooses own wallet, occasionally carries physical cards). Exclude test submissions.
- **Finished:** Eligible respondents who completed and submitted all required survey questions. Exclude tests and known repeat participants.
