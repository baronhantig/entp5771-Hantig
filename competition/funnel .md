# Milestone I: Demand — Recruiting Funnel

## Recruiting channels and measurement

I recruited respondents through two channels: my University of Utah fraternity and multiple Reddit posts, including a post in r/SampleSize. Both channels used the same Google Form link, and the form did not separately record the recruitment source.

For the fraternity, I counted 124 people approached and identified 9 completed responses from members I knew had taken the survey. For Reddit, the updated Post Insights showed 411 post views, up by 100 from 311 at the previous checkpoint. A view is exposure to the post, not proof that a unique person opened the survey. I used no other recruitment channels. The original 30 usable Reddit responses were attributed by elimination, not individual source tracking. I confirmed that the 2 additional respondents came from Reddit and completed the survey, bringing Reddit finishes to 32. The 411 views are the recorded Reddit exposure count; views from any additional posts not included in that count are unmeasured.

## Funnel counts

Updated Sunday, November 8, 2026, with 100 additional Reddit views and 2 additional submitted responses. Both new responses are confirmed completed surveys from Reddit.

*The 44 figure counts submitted Google Form response rows, not every person who opened or began filling out the form. Because Google Forms did not track incomplete, unsubmitted attempts, actual starts may have been higher.

The original export contains 42 submitted rows. Two additional responses bring the reported total to 44. Both additional Reddit respondents completed the survey, bringing the passed-screener and usable-finish totals to 41 based on my confirmation. Of the original submissions, three failed or did not complete the screening requirements and had no willingness-to-pay answer. Of the original 42 submissions, the remaining 39 passed the three screeners and provided numeric willingness-to-pay answers, including eight genuine $0 answers. One eligible respondent left the product-familiarity question blank, so “finished” here means a complete usable response for the demand analysis, not necessarily every optional field answered. See `cleaning.md`.

## Conversion measures and limitations

| Measure | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Fraternity usable finishes per approached | 9 / 124 | 7.3% |
| Reddit attributed usable finishes per recorded post view | 32 / 411 | 7.8% |
| Combined usable finishes per recorded exposure | 41 / 535 | 7.7% |
| Usable responses per submitted form | 41 / 44 | 93.2% |

These ratios are descriptive, not independently verified funnel-stage conversion rates. In particular, Reddit post views may include repeat views, and the 124 fraternity approaches and 411 Reddit views are not necessarily comparable measures of unique people. Because both channels used the same link without a source question, I cannot report channel-specific Starts or Passed Screener without inventing numbers.

## Fraternity — three-sentence checkpoint

Of 124 fraternity members approached, 9 provided usable completed surveys, but I cannot see how many started the form or were screened out. The 7.3% usable-response-to-approach ratio suggests that getting members to take the survey was a larger observable obstacle than obtaining usable answers from those who submitted it, although the missing stages prevent a precise diagnosis. In the next ten days, I would recruit members face-to-face and record how many actually open the form, pass the screeners, and finish.

## Reddit — three-sentence checkpoint

I recruited through multiple Reddit posts, recorded 411 views, and attributed 32 usable responses to Reddit: 30 inferred from the original responses and 2 additional confirmed completions, while total Reddit-specific starts and screener passes remain unmeasured. The 7.8% attributed usable-response-to-recorded-view ratio suggests the post generated some participation, although views are not unique people and I cannot locate the exact point of drop-off. In the next ten days, I would track new post insights and use a separate channel-specific link or a recruitment-source question to measure the Reddit funnel more accurately.

## Interpretation

The updated funnel records 535 approaches or post views across the two channels and 44 submitted responses, including 41 eligible, usable responses. It is not possible to reconstruct every stage by channel from a shared Google Form link, and the 44 submitted forms should not be misrepresented as all people who started the survey. These limitations matter when interpreting the reachable population and comparing the stated willingness to pay with the Ohsnap campaign's actual backers.
