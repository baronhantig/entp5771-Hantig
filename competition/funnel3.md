# Recruitment Funnel — Wallet Comparison Survey

## Channel: Reddit

Baron shared the second wallet-comparison survey through **one Reddit post**. The post received **68 views**. Google Forms recorded **2 submitted responses**, both of which passed the three eligibility screeners (18 or older, personally chooses a wallet/card holder, and carries physical cards at least occasionally).

| Funnel stage | Count | Measurement note |
|---|---:|---|
| Approached | 68 | Reddit post views; views are not necessarily unique people |
| Started | Unknown | Survey opens or partial starts were not measured |
| Passed screener | 2 confirmed | Both submitted responses met all three eligibility conditions; any unsubmitted screener attempts are unknown |
| Finished | 2 | Completed Google Forms submissions |

**Observed post-view-to-completion ratio:** 2 / 68 = **2.9%**. This is not a survey-start-to-completion conversion rate because the number of starts is unavailable.

## Three-sentence funnel interpretation

The largest uncertainty in the funnel is between the 68 Reddit post views and the 2 completed surveys because the number of people who opened or started the form was not tracked. This means I cannot tell whether the low number of completions came mainly from limited interest in clicking the survey, screening failures among people who did not submit, or people abandoning it after starting. Over the next ten days, I would recruit eligible respondents directly, track how many people receive the invitation, and record starts and completions separately so I can identify where respondents are lost.

## Comparison with Survey 1

The first survey's fraternity channel recorded **124 approached, 9 started, 6 passed screening, and 6 finished** (6/124 = **4.8%** completed per approach). Its Reddit channel recorded **247 post views, 2 starts, 2 passed screening, and 2 finished** (2/247 = **0.8%** completed per view). The second survey's single Reddit post produced **2 finishes from 68 views (2.9%)**, higher than the first Reddit post's observed view-to-completion ratio, but with only two completions in each survey and unknown second-survey starts, this does not establish improved recruiting effectiveness.

## Data limitations

- This funnel covers the **second wallet-comparison survey**, not the earlier survey.
- Only one Reddit post was used; no other recruitment channels were reported.
- The 68 views may include repeat views and do not establish 68 unique people.
- No unsubmitted or partial Google Forms responses were available.
- This is a small, nonrandom sample and should not be treated as representative of wallet buyers.
